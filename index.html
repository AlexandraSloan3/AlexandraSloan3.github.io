Foundations

Learning Approach

Foundations are studied just-in-time and literature-driven: rather than completing each subject exhaustively before starting research, I work directly from the primary literature — beginning with the original KAN paper and extending outward to related theoretical work — identifying each unfamiliar concept as it appears and studying only the material needed to understand it rigorously. Core concepts (e.g., neural network fundamentals) are studied in depth; peripheral concepts are studied to the depth the literature requires. This keeps preparation tightly scoped to what the research actually uses, rather than following a fixed curriculum end-to-end.

Primary references drawn on as needed: MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra, Mu Li's Dive into Deep Learning, Thomas' Calculus / MIT 18.01–18.02, and MIT 6.041/18.05 Probability.
